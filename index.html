AS-3 / S S Saziya Creation

S S Saziya Creation is a Surat-based garment manufacturing company established in 2015. AS-3 is its customer-facing apparel identity, specializing in the development and supply of premium ethnic and contemporary garments across men's, women's and kids' categories.
